Artifact Name: Chemical Shift Artifact
Modality: ASL MRI
Category: Physical

Short Description:
Chemical shift artifact occurs due to differences in resonance frequencies between fat and water protons.

How It Appears in Image:
Fat and water boundaries appear misaligned, creating bright or dark bands at tissue interfaces.

Primary Cause:
Frequency differences between fat and water signals during image encoding.

Clinical Impact:
May lead to incorrect interpretation of tissue boundaries.

Severity Level:
Low to Moderate

Common Fix:
Use fat suppression techniques or increase receiver bandwidth.
