Artifact Name: Parallel Imaging Artifact
Modality: ASL MRI
Category: Reconstruction

Short Description:
Parallel imaging artifacts occur when accelerated imaging techniques reconstruct incomplete data inaccurately.

How It Appears in Image:
Images may show noise amplification or residual aliasing patterns.

Primary Cause:
High acceleration factors or inaccurate coil sensitivity estimation.

Clinical Impact:
Reduces signal-to-noise ratio and affects reliability of perfusion quantification.

Severity Level:
Moderate

Common Fix:
Reduce acceleration factor or improve coil sensitivity calibration.
