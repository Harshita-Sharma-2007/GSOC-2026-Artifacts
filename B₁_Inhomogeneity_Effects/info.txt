Artifact Name: B₁ Inhomogeneity
Modality: ASL MRI
Category: RF Field Artifact

Short Description:
B₁ inhomogeneity occurs when the radiofrequency transmit field is uneven across the imaging region.

How It Appears in Image:
Some regions appear brighter or darker than expected due to uneven flip angles.

Primary Cause:
Uneven RF transmission caused by coil design limitations or patient anatomy.

Clinical Impact:
Leads to inaccurate signal intensity and unreliable perfusion measurements.

Severity Level:
Moderate

Common Fix:
Use RF calibration techniques or advanced coil designs to improve RF field uniformity.
